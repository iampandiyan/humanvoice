Privacy Policy for Where is my people

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Where is my people ("we," "our," or "the App") is a family location-sharing app for iOS and Android, published by Karuppasamy Pandiyan Marimuthu under the name Humanvoice. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

In short: The App shares your location only with the people in your own family circle. In addition to your latest location, we keep a short location history (currently your last 3 days with location activity) so the members of your family circle can see your recent movement, as a list and as a route drawn on a map; older history is deleted automatically. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising SDKs (the map provider's own usage data is described in Section 4). You can export your data or delete your account at any time from Settings inside the App.

1. Information We Collect

To provide the service we collect the following information:

  • Account details: your first and last name and your email address. The App does not ask for your date of birth. Your password is stored as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see it.
  • Details from earlier versions: earlier versions of the App asked for a date of birth and, for younger users, a parent or guardian's email address and the time they confirmed. The App no longer asks for these and does not use them. If your account was created with an earlier version, any such details still on file are deleted with your account.
  • Location: your device's precise location, including while the App is in the background, together with its accuracy, speed, direction of travel and the time it was recorded. We store your most recent location on our servers, plus a short-term location history (currently the last 3 days on which you had location activity, per family) so your family can see your recent movement; older history rows are deleted automatically. Your family can look at this history one day at a time, either as a list of places or as your route drawn on a map. If your phone is offline, the newest unsent location is held in encrypted storage on your phone until it can be sent.
  • Device status: battery level and whether the phone is charging, your phone's platform (iOS or Android), and a push-notification token so we can send the App a notification.
  • Phones and sign-ins (account activity): for each phone your account is used on, the phone's model name and operating system version, the App version, and a random installation ID that the App creates on that phone. We also record when you sign in or out, when your account moves to a different phone, when location sharing is switched off or back on (as reported by the App), and how many fake-location readings the App blocked. Each stored location also records which of your phones sent it and when our servers received it. This log holds no IP address and no place. Phone names are whatever the phone reports about itself.
  • Time zone: the time zone of your phone, or the one you choose in Settings (for example "Asia/Kolkata"), stored with your account so that days and times are shown correctly.
  • Family circle information: the family circle you belong to, your role in it, and its invite code. You can use the App without a circle: until you create or join one, the App does not collect or send your location. If you share an invite, the message contains a link to a page on humanvoice.in (hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log the visit) with the invite code in it; nobody is added to a circle without confirming in the App.
  • Places: places that you or your family add (for example Home, Work, School), each with a name, type, map position and radius. The App can show a place badge when you are at one of these places.
  • Note for family (optional): a short message of up to 50 characters that you can write for your family. It is visible to the members of your family circle.
  • Map activity: a timestamp that shows a family member currently has the live map open. The App uses this to update locations faster while someone is looking, and to save battery when nobody is.
  • Technical data: our hosting and security providers process standard technical information such as your IP address when your phone connects to the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Showing your family where you are on the live map and in the family list (including battery level and the place you are at).
  • Showing recent movement: letting your family look back over your recent location history, day by day, as a list and as a route on a map. Days and times are shown in the time zone of the person looking.
  • Running your account: signing you in, confirming your email address, resetting your password, and keeping your family circle together.
  • Keeping one account on one phone: your account can be signed in on one phone at a time. If you sign in on another phone, we ask whether to sign the first one out. When your account moves, we notify the phone that was signed out, send you a security email, and let your family circle know that a family member signed in on a different phone. This is also why History can say which phone sent each location.
  • Saving battery: adjusting how often your phone reports its location depending on whether someone is watching and whether you are moving.
  • Protecting the service: preventing automated sign-ups and abuse, and warning you if you choose a password that has appeared in a public data breach.
  • Legal compliance: keeping records needed to answer data requests.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Your location (current and recent history), battery level, current place and note are visible to the active members of your family circle and to no one else. Any active member can browse your location history one day at a time, and several members' routes can be shown together on one map. When you leave a family circle, or are removed from it, your location history for that circle is deleted immediately, and a short marker that you are no longer in the circle stays visible to its members for 3 days. The same members can also see your account activity (Section 1), the list of phones your account has been used on and when it was signed in or out or moved to a different phone, from the day you joined the circle. That log is informational: it records what phones and our servers reported, and it cannot show who was holding a phone or where a person was. Changing or reinstalling a phone is normal. We do not sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or data brokers. Our service providers (Section 4) process data only to run the App for us.

4. Service Providers

We rely on the following providers to run the App:

  • Supabase: database and account sign-in (authentication).
  • Cloudflare: hosts the App's server (Cloudflare Workers) and provides the Turnstile bot check that runs invisibly when you sign in, sign up or ask for a password-reset code. Turnstile reads a few technical signals from your device, such as your IP address and connection and browser details, only to tell people from automated bots. See Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications to your phone, including a silent message that asks the App to update its location, and notices that your account was signed out because it moved to another phone or that a family member signed in on a different phone.
  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES): sends account emails such as the confirmation link, password-reset codes and a security notice when your account moves to another phone.
  • Mapbox: displays the map and answers place searches. Your phone requests map images from Mapbox, and when you search for an address while adding a place, the text you type and a nearby reference point (to rank results) are sent to Mapbox's search service. Mapbox's map software also sends de-identified location and usage data to Mapbox to improve its maps; this is Mapbox's own collection and is not used for advertising. You can switch it off with the information (i) button on the map, then "Mapbox Telemetry". See Mapbox's privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google (your phone's built-in geocoder): turns coordinates into street and area names on the member card, the family list and the location history. This lookup is made by your phone's operating system.
  • Have I Been Pwned: when you choose a password, only the first five characters of a one-way hash of it are sent, to check whether it appears in known breaches. Your password itself is never sent.

If you tap "Open in Google Maps" on a member card or on a location history entry, Google Maps opens with that location and is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

5. Device Permissions

  • Location (including "Always" / "Allow all the time"): required to share your location with your family while the App is in the background. Before your phone's permission prompt, the App shows a message that says what is collected, that it happens even when the App is closed or not in use, and who can see it. Whenever the App is using your location in the background, Android shows a notification (the App stops using GPS while you stay still, and the notification goes away until you move again). You can change or revoke the permission in your device settings, but sharing will stop.
  • Notifications: used for the App's push messages and background service.
  • Internet / network: required to send and receive locations.
  • Motion / activity detection (where your device supports it): used to detect when you start moving, so the App can stop using GPS while you stay still.

6. Data Storage and Security

Data is sent to our servers over encrypted connections (TLS) and is encrypted at rest by our database provider. On your phone, the App keeps its random installation ID in the device's secure storage and a small encrypted local store (AES-256, with the key held in the same secure storage) for the newest unsent location, the last family snapshot, and the street and area names your phone has already looked up (so the same place is not looked up again); all of it is erased when you sign out or delete your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Data Retention, Export and Deletion

  • Location history is limited to your most recent 3 days with location activity (currently), per family. Days on which your phone recorded no location do not count, and older history is deleted automatically by a daily cleanup job. If you stop using the App, your last 3 days with activity stay until you delete your account or leave the family circle; leaving or being removed from a circle deletes your history for that circle immediately. Your latest location is also kept separately so your family can always see where you are right now.
  • Account activity (Section 1) follows the same rule as location history: it is kept for as long as your most recent 3 days with location activity, or for 3 calendar days if that is longer, and is deleted with them; there is no separate longer retention. Phones that nothing refers to any more are removed at the same time. We do not promise that any record exists for longer than this.
  • Legal requests: we do not give your data to private parties. We answer valid legal process only, and we can only provide what we still hold under the rules above.
  • Export your data: in the App go to Settings → Export my data to save a copy of the information we hold about you as a file.
  • Delete your account: in the App go to Settings → Delete account. Your profile, your latest location and location history, places you created, family memberships, account activity log and the list of your phones are removed from our database. If you created a family circle and other members are still in it, ownership passes to the member who joined next; if you were the only member, the circle and its places are removed.
  • Without the App: you can request deletion on our account deletion page.
Delete your account and data: How to delete your Where is my people account

Requests made outside the App are processed within 7 business days.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or California law) you may have the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. You can exercise most of these rights inside the App (Settings), or by emailing us using the contact details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. By creating an account you confirm that you are 18 or older. The App does not ask for your age or date of birth. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

10. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may store and process information in countries other than your own. We choose providers that protect data in transit and at rest, and we process your information only as described in this policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes, for example if we add a new provider or feature. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the App.
