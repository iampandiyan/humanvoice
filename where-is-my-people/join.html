Join a circle on Where is my people

Someone invited you to their family circle. Follow the three steps below.

Your invite code

  1. Get the app. The app is being tested and is not on the store yet. If you were invited to the test, use the link from your invitation.
  2. Create your account with your email and a password, and confirm your email.
  3. Tap "I have an invite code" and enter the code above. You will be asked to confirm before you join.

Only the members of a circle can see each other's location, and only after each person joins. You can leave a circle at any time.
