Family location sharing: a privacy checklist

Before using any family location app, check who can see your location, what the company stores, whether it shows ads or sells data, and whether you can delete your account and data yourself.

By Karuppasamy Pandiyan Marimuthu · Last updated 2026-09-24

Check a family location app in 5 steps

  1. Find out exactly who can see your location and how someone is added to the group.
  2. Read what the app stores about you and for how long.
  3. Check that the app does not show ads or sell your data.
  4. Make sure you can export and delete your data from inside the app.
  5. Review the phone permissions and battery settings the app needs to work reliably.

Talk to your family first

Location sharing works best when everyone agrees. Explain why you are sharing it, decide together who is in the circle, and respect requests to leave.
