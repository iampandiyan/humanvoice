How apartment gate vehicle logging works

Register resident vehicles once, then have the guard scan each number plate at the gate. Known vehicles match instantly, unknown ones are logged as visitors, and every entry and exit is recorded with the operator's name.

By Karuppasamy Pandiyan Marimuthu · Last updated 2026-09-24

Set up gate vehicle logging in 5 steps

  1. Add residents with their flat numbers and vehicle numbers.
  2. Give each staff member a role: Admin, Operator or Viewer, and keep Admin for trusted staff only.
  3. At the gate, the operator scans the vehicle's number plate with the phone camera.
  4. If the plate matches a resident or regular visitor, log the entry. If not, record the visitor's flat number and purpose of visit.
  5. Review the entry and exit log when needed, and export it to Excel or CSV for the association.

How ANPR Smart Gate Tracker does it

ANPR Smart Gate Tracker is an Android app for apartment communities. Number-plate text recognition runs on the phone, and the app is built to work mainly offline. It keeps resident and visitor records, a searchable entry and exit log with the operator's name for audits, Admin, Operator and Viewer roles, Excel and CSV exports, and optional backup to the admin's Google Drive.

Good practice for communities

  • Tell residents and visitors that vehicle numbers are recorded at the gate.
  • Give only trusted staff the Admin role.
  • Back up regularly, and delete records you no longer need.

The app stores data on the device. Read the privacy policy for details on what is collected.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes, it is designed to work primarily offline. Internet is needed for Google sign-in, license checks and Google Drive backups.

Is it available on iPhone?

No. ANPR Smart Gate Tracker is an Android app.

Can different staff have different access?

Yes. The app has Admin, Operator and Viewer roles, so you can control what each person does within the same community.
